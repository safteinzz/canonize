# workspaces.md

What an agent does in every workspace, read before anything else there. It lives in your canon, so every workspace gets it, the ones linked later included: canonize names it in the CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md it writes at a workspace's top.

The timeline below is an example to keep or replace.

## Timeline

Keep `TIMELINE.md` in the folder you were started in, so a session that is lost leaves its work on disk and the next one picks it up.

- Before anything else, read it when it is there, and go on from its last entry.
- After each piece of work, before you answer, append an entry: the date and time, what was asked, what was done (files written, commands run, decisions and the reason for each), and what is next. One entry per piece of work, newest last, a few lines each.
- When you stop mid-task, the last entry says where you stopped and what remains.
- Never rewrite or delete an entry. Correct one with a new entry.
- CONTEXT.md at the workspace's top is for what every session there needs to know. TIMELINE.md is for what happened in this folder.
